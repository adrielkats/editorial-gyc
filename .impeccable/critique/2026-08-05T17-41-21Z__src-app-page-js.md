## Design Specificity Verdict

**Category-interchangeable — the interface does not carry the wood/cream furniture identity.** PRODUCT.md commits to wood/cream aesthetics, but that identity exists on exactly one element: the footer `#F4EFE8` (globals.css:754). Everything else is generic agency chrome: navy `#1a1a2e` header, gray `#f4f4f9` body, white cards, emoji sector icons (🪑🪞📚), and a full Argentine flag as the hero. The hero flag is national/regional specificity, not product specificity — equally at home on a bakery or a plumber. Wood tokens (`#8B4513`/`#DAA520`/`#C0C0C0`, globals.css:230-240) read as category tags, not material. Emojis render inconsistently on low-end Android.

## Overall Impression

The funnel architecture (grid → detail → WhatsApp) is right and the performance engineering is genuinely product-true — but the homepage wastes the single most persuasive asset (26 real product photos) and buries the buyer in corporate prose and a national flag. The biggest opportunity: put real products on the home and make the wood/cream identity actual.

## What's Working

1. **Public grid is genuinely minimal** — whole-card `<Link>` (image + name only) is one large tappable target, ideal for low-end phones (ProductosCategoria.js:66-73).
2. **Progressive disclosure is right** — grid → detail → prominent pre-filled WhatsApp CTA matches the stated funnel (ProductosCategoria.js:39, [id]/page.js:69-72).
3. **Performance is product-true** — Cloudinary `w_400/f_auto/q_80` grid / `w_800` detail is built for the gama-baja audience (ProductosCategoria.js:29-35).

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Homepage shows zero products.** Funnel is catalog→detail→WhatsApp but home is flag + corporate prose + sector cards; 26 real photos sit two clicks deep. *Why:* the strongest asset never reaches the highest-traffic page. *Fix:* add a "Destacados" product strip above the about text, cut about to 2 lines, demote the flag. → /impeccable layout
2. **[P1] Price and stock are admin-only.** PRODUCT.md says they live on public detail, but `{esAdmin && ...}` gates Precio/Stock/Categoría ([id]/page.js:61-67). Buyers hit WhatsApp blind. *Fix:* show them publicly, or deliberately hide and say "Consultá precio por WhatsApp" — pick one, update the other. → /impeccable clarify
3. **[P1] "Admin" login sits in the public nav with a hardcoded password in client JS** (Header.js:7-8, 44-56). *Why:* breaks buyer mental model and exposes a credential in the bundle. *Fix:* remove from nav; trigger via unobvious gesture or a routed `/admin` not linked. → /impeccable clarify
4. **[P2] Espejos and Libros promoted but empty** (sector cards + header links, zero products). *Why:* dead ends erode trust exactly where discovery is promised. *Fix:* hide empty categories or label "Próximamente". → /impeccable clarify
5. **[P2] Navigation recovery is fragile.** "Volver" is `router.back()` with no breadcrumb; shared WhatsApp links land on detail with a broken way back. *Fix:* `<Link>` to category home + breadcrumb. → /impeccable adapt

## Persona Red Flags

- **Casey (distracted mobile, low-end):** Home needs scrolling past a 350px flag + ~10 lines of prose before any actionable card; then 3 full page-loads (sector → grid → detail). Wrapped header nav can drop "Admin" alone onto a second line. No search/filter over 26 one-column cards. Emoji icons can render as empty boxes on old Android. Floating WhatsApp (56px, bottom-right) competes with thumb scroll.
- **Jordan (first-timer):** No above-the-fold answer to "can I buy this here?"; corporate boilerplate about text; an unlabeled "Admin" button invites a wasted tap; clicking Espejos/Libros lands on "No hay productos todavía" → feels broken; detail shows no price → Jordan guesses the process.
- **Sam (accessibility):** WhatsApp float is emoji-only with no `aria-label` (page.js:67-69). Admin modal has no `role="dialog"`, no focus trap, no Escape close (Header.js:60-71). Wrong password uses `alert()`. Decorative flag sun emoji not `aria-hidden`. Empty-state gray `#888` on `#f4f4f9` ~3:1 borderline. Hover-only states, no focus-visible.

## Minor Observations

- Navy chrome vs wood/cream footer is the identity contradiction made visible — "identidad" is only ever a footer swatch.
- No `catch` on any fetch; failed loads hang on "Cargando...".
- WhatsApp pre-fill includes a product link in grid (ProductosCategoria.js:39) but not on detail ([id]/page.js:36) — inconsistent.
- No `og:`/social meta and no favicon — sharing a product on WhatsApp yields a bare preview.
- Stray `<strong> Corrientes</strong>` orphan space (page.js:33).
- Fixed 180px contain grid images letterbox inconsistently.
- Grid is 1 column on phones — correct, but 26 items = very long scroll.
- Five button colors (verde/azul/rojo/naranja/gris) leak dashboard aesthetic into the catalog.

## Questions to Consider

- If the funnel is catalog→detail→WhatsApp, why does the home show zero products and two paragraphs of corporate text?
- Is hiding the price intentional (informal negotiation) or an implementation leak? Code and PRODUCT.md contradict each other.
- The hero is the Argentine flag and the brand is "wood/cream" — who is that hero for, the owner or the buyer?
- A buyer shares a product link; the recipient gets a bare page, no image preview, and a "Volver" that dead-ends. Is that the acquisition loop you want for the primary channel?
